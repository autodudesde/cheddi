# cat=cheddiweb/websources; type=string; label=Trusted sources:Comma-separated domains web research is restricted to, e.g. "gesetze-im-internet.de,ihk.de". Empty (default, recommended) searches the open web. This is a hard filter, not a ranking preference: a question whose answer lives nowhere on the list returns no sources at all, and an assistant with no sources falls back on what it remembers. Use it only where the subject really is confined to a known set of sources, keep the list short, and review it - a forgotten list narrows every research from then on. The block list below is the safer instrument for most installations. Takes precedence over it: both providers accept only one of the two. At most 10 domains are sent; anything beyond that is dropped and logged.

# cat=cheddiweb/webscope; type=string; label=Country override:Two-letter ISO country the results are localised for. Empty (default) takes the region of the site's default language, which is right for almost every installation. Set it when the editorial team writes for a different market than the site's own.

# cat=cheddiweb/webscope; type=int+; label=Searches per turn:How many searches one turn may run. 0 (default) leaves it to the server. Raising it buys depth on comparison questions and costs a flat fee per search; lowering it caps a runaway research turn.
